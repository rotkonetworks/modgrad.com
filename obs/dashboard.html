Workspace

Each panel is a query (shown as chips; "text" edits it directly). Drag series from the list onto the board or a panel, or use their + buttons; drag a panel title onto another panel to merge, onto its edge to reorder. "expand" opens a panel full-screen. Ctrl+K: commands. Saved in this browser; export to share. "Reset to default" builds a board from metric naming conventions, else from the History view below.

History

First selected run is drawn as a solid area, the rest as 1 px lines. Legend statistics are over the raw (unsmoothed) values. Single-point series are drawn as squares.
Forecast (opt-in): dashed ghost and faint band per run, from one-step ridge regression on lag windows rolled forward, fitted to the raw values. Lookback, ridge strength, scaler, input-noise and pooling over the other plotted runs of the metric are chosen on early backtest origins; the MAE shown is on the later origins only, against a last-value forecast on the same points. When the ridge does not beat last-value on both splits the ghost is the last value. Band: 10 to 90% of backtest residuals per horizon. Forecasts are not counted in the legend statistics.

Across runs: metric over step and config

Cells where a run has no value at that step are gaps: hatched in the heatmap, marked x on the base of the surface. Nothing is interpolated. Runs sharing a config value are averaged (count in the tooltip). Click a cell or vertex for slices.
